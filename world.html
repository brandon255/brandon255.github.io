The hunt

How a piece gets found

1Build your buddy

Dovetail pieces, no glue, no tools. The last piece clicks — and your buddy wakes up.

2Get the call

Your buddy tells you a piece of your island is waiting at a maker's shop, a studio, a chapter breakfast.

3Go together

You and your grown-up go somewhere new — a real creative place, maybe the first time you've ever been inside one.

4Wake the piece

Scan it. It joins your island. You post the find. Somewhere far away, another kid sees it — and goes hunting.

Pen pals

A pen pal on every continent

🌐The all-engineered translator

Every message travels with a live translation under it — engineered in-house, running local-first. Kids write in their own language; the buddy carries the words across. Nobody needs to "know English" to have friends in Berlin, Lagos, or Mexico City.

✉️Slow mail, fast mail

Voice notes, drawings, and photos of builds — plus the occasional real postcard when a chapter mails a kid an actual piece of the world.

🔒Grown-up safe

Local-first and private: children's data stays on the family's device, nothing egresses, every pen-pal connection is grown-up approved. The buddy talks — and never phones home.
